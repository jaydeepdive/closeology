Priority leads

Every British Columbia, Ontario, Yukon, Newfoundland & Labrador, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Northwest Territories, Nova Scotia, Quebec and Nunavut lead ranked on one common scale — driven by in-situ metal value, deposit size, development status, open ground, drilling on record and exploration spend. A score of 80 means the same thing in every jurisdiction. Each lead shows exactly why it sits where it does.

Project Closeology — a sister project to The Deep Dive. Screening signals over official data (BC MTO/MINFILE, Ontario LIO/MDI); metal values are approximate and refreshed daily. Always confirm every claim and boundary in the official title system before staking — this is decision support, not staking advice.
